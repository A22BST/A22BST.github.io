01  Featured · Bachelor thesis

Bachelor thesis · DTU · team of 2

Terrain-Adaptive Landing Gear for a VTOL Drone

A drone that lands on a slope or on rocks ends up tilted. We built landing gear with three legs that extend until each foot touches the ground, so the drone settles level.

One small motor per leg turns three nested 3D-printed screws, and a time-of-flight sensor decides whether the legs need to deploy. I owned the mechatronics: electronics, ESP32 firmware, a custom KiCad board and a PX4/Gazebo simulation.
